LearningXY Facebook Bot: Data Deletion

Last updated: 23 September 2026

How to request deletion

Email keep-caviar-shimmy@duck.com with the subject Facebook bot data deletion request.

Include your Facebook profile link or name and, if available, a link to the relevant comment or post. Provide only enough information for us to identify the records concerned. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

What happens next

We will check what information we hold, request proportionate verification if needed, and delete information covered by your request unless there is a lawful reason to retain it. We will explain the result, including if we hold no relevant information, and respond within the applicable legal timeframe.

The current test bot displays events in a local terminal and does not save them to files or a database. A deletion request can include clearing any relevant terminal history still available to us. Privacy request emails are handled separately.

Information on Facebook

Deleting information held by the bot does not automatically delete your original Facebook comments or messages, or records held by service providers. You can manage your Facebook content using Facebook's own controls.

Read the bot privacy notice for more information.
