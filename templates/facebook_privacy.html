LearningXY Facebook Bot Privacy Notice

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Who we are

Michalis Karaolis operates the LearningXY Facebook bot and is responsible for its handling of personal data. Contact: keep-caviar-shimmy@duck.com.

This notice covers the Facebook bot. Website bookings, payments and teaching services are outside its scope.

Information the bot receives

Meta sends notifications about activity covered by the bot's subscriptions on the LearningXY Facebook Page. Depending on the event and permissions, these may include comment or post text, names, Facebook identifiers, post and comment identifiers, timestamps and the type of activity.

The current test version receives and displays these events. It does not yet send automatic messages.

Purpose and lawful basis

We use this information to test, operate and troubleshoot the Page integration. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in maintaining a working Page communication service, balanced against the rights and expectations of people interacting with the Page.

The current bot does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about people.

Storage and retention

The current bot displays events in a local terminal. It does not save those events to log files or a database. Displayed information can remain in terminal scrollback until that history is cleared or the terminal session is closed. Stopping the bot alone does not necessarily clear that history.

Comments and messages may remain on Facebook under Meta's own retention arrangements. Emails sent to us about privacy requests are separate records; we retain them only as necessary to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

Services involved

Meta supplies the Facebook events. Cloudflare Tunnel currently carries webhook requests to the local bot. Privacy request emails pass through DuckDuckGo Email Protection and the receiving email service. These providers may process information in other countries and maintain their own service records.

See the Meta privacy policy, Cloudflare privacy policy and DuckDuckGo Email Protection privacy policy for their processing practices.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal data we hold about you, and portability where applicable, by contacting keep-caviar-shimmy@duck.com.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, including by emailing us at the address above.

You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or your relevant data protection authority.

Deletion and changes

See our data deletion instructions. We will update this notice when the bot's data handling changes, including before introducing saved event records or automatic messages.
